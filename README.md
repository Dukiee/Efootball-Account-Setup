# Efootball-Account-Setup
A Guide on how to download and set your efootball account 

## How to Install the eFootball App

- Open the **Play Store** or **App Store**.
- Search for **"eFootball"**.
- Click **Install** and wait for the installation to complete.

## How to Set Up Your eFootball Account

- **Launch the eFootball app** on your device.
- **Select your preferred language.**
- **Select your country/region.**
- **Read and accept the Terms and Conditions.**
- put your **email address** and do the necessary authentication 
- **Choose your username.**
- **Download the necessary data** and you're good to go!
